## Description (max 4000)
Backprop teaches machine learning the way it actually clicks: by playing with it.

Every lesson takes about five minutes. Read a short idea, try it in a live simulation, then check yourself with a few quick questions. Build a daily streak and watch your XP grow.

LEARN BY DOING
• Drag points around and watch a regression line refit in real time
• Roll a ball down a loss curve and see what a learning rate really does, including when training blows up
• Every concept is followed by a quick quiz with clear explanations
• A curated reading list of the best free ML books, papers and courses

99 LESSONS IN 4 TRACKS
• Data foundations: descriptive stats, probability, distributions, inference
• Classic ML: regression, gradient descent, classification, trees and ensembles, clustering, evaluation
• Neural networks: neurons, backpropagation, training tricks, CNNs, sequence models
• Modern AI: embeddings, attention, transformers, LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation

BUILT FOR BUSY PEOPLE
• 5-minute lessons that fit a coffee break
• Daily streaks and XP to keep you going
• Works offline, with no account needed

START FREE
The first two chapters of every track are free. Backprop Pro unlocks every chapter and all future tracks.

## App Review notes
Backprop is an ML/data science learning app. No sign-in is required.

To test the subscription: finish onboarding, and the paywall appears (it can be closed with the X button). Free content is chapters 1–2 of each track, and chapter 3 onward shows a lock and opens the paywall. Purchases go through StoreKit via RevenueCat. Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription are in the Settings tab.

All lesson content is bundled in the app. The app doesn't download code.
