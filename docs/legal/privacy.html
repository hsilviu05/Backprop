Backprop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Backprop ("the app") is an app for learning machine learning and data science. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: we don't ask for your name or email, we don't show ads, and we don't track you.

Data stored on your device

Your lesson progress, quiz scores, streaks, XP and onboarding answers (your goal, level and daily time) are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To manage subscriptions, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app (not linked to your name, email or Apple Account),
  • your purchase and subscription history for this app (product, price, dates, status),
  • basic device information such as device model, iOS version, app version, country/storefront and IP address.

We use this data only to unlock the features you paid for, restore purchases, and understand aggregate subscription metrics. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Links to other websites

The Reading tab links to third-party websites, which open in an in-app browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with data brokers.
  • No account is required, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or microphone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because the app uses an anonymous ID, include your App Store order ID (from your Apple purchase receipt email) when you contact us so we can find the related subscription records. You can manage or cancel subscriptions anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact
