Backprop Support

Help for the Backprop iOS app

Contact

For questions, bug reports or feedback, open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iOS version and the app version shown at the bottom of Settings.

Subscriptions

  • Restore Pro on a new device: open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Cancel or change your plan: open Settings in the app and tap Manage subscription, or go to the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

Lesson progress, streaks and XP are stored only on your device. There is no account, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.
